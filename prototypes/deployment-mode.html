Deployment mode — interactive prototype

The canvas has two modes. Live is the environment as it is now. Pick a deployment from the floating Deployments bar at the bottom and the canvas becomes that deployment: nodes transform to show build → deploy and a log tail, and the service panel becomes Railway-style Details · Build logs · Deploy logs. Press Review changes → Deploy and you stay in live; the switcher shows the running deployment. There is no Deployments page or sidebar item.
