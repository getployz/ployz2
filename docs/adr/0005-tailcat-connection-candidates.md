# Management Capability connections replace the hosted relay protocol

SSH and the Management Capability share the existing Machine RPC Connector and identity confirmation. Cloud repurposes `organizationMachine` as encrypted backend-only connection metadata, scoped by Organization, Machine and current pairing; it is neither membership nor presence. This supersedes the protocol and slot decisions in 0001/0002 and the relay-hold completion condition in 0003, avoiding a second catalog or presence service.

The authenticated founder publishes its candidate before completion. The enrollment claim fixes the intended Machine and pairing generation, while its Management Capability may rotate. An authenticated retry may replace the capability only after shared negotiation verifies the intended Machine; publication rechecks the current claim and unchanged saved candidate after verification, without holding a database lock across the network call. The current claim records the intended Machine as well as its public key; shared negotiation must confirm that Machine before transactional first-connect admission. Matching retries resume even after a lost completion response. Candidate absence and transport failure never authorize founder transfer or reset; reset remains unavailable until endpoint revocation can be confirmed.

The greenfield database history is a single baseline containing the current schema. It initializes fresh databases; it does not migrate existing pairings or authorize their reset. Existing databases require a separately planned cutover. Every new pairing must name a non-null 32-hex founding Machine ID.

The greenfield cutover removes the custom relay process, held registrations, tenant slots, and relay release/deployment artifacts. Supported Machine RPC connections use local sockets, SSH, or the in-process iroh management transport: the daemon serves Machine RPC on its Management Identity, and clients dial it through the self-hosted Ployz Relay, upgrading to direct paths when they work. Removing a pairing clears the accepted client key in one mutation and closes its connections; the removal response, or an identity refusal on retry, is the confirmation, and an unreachable Machine stays unconfirmed. Historical relay rationale remains in the explicitly superseded 0001/0002 records.
